# Section skill: About (AVS Infra)

## Job
Establish credibility fast — who built this, since 2017, and why "on schedule"
is the brand's real promise (City Palace: 18 months, City Square: 24 months).
This is a trust checkpoint right after the hero's big claim, not a mission-statement
wall of text.

## Structure — asymmetric two-column, ink section (band change from paper)
- Left column (40%): "The Measure" tick — "02 — THE DEVELOPER" mono label, then a
  short display headline: "Built on schedule, since 2017." Below it, 2 short
  paragraphs of real brochure copy (est. 2017, vision to transcend expectations,
  premium commercial focus), condensed and rewritten in plain active voice.
- Right column (60%): a **stat ledger** — not icon cards, a table-like list of
  4 rows separated by hairlines, each row: big mono number + label, e.g.
  "2017 — Founded" / "18 MO — City Palace delivered" / "24 MO — City Square
  delivered" / "3 — Projects completed or underway". Numbers in brass, labels in
  paper/70%. This reuses the measured/timeline idea from the hero without repeating
  the same visual device (rail vs. ledger — related family, not identical).

## Background
`--ink` solid section (first dark band = rhythm change from hero/paper). Faint
brass hairline grid or single thin diagonal line as texture, very subtle, no imagery
competing with the stat ledger.

## Motion
Simple scroll-reveal: left column fades/slides up first, ledger rows stagger in
one after another (80ms apart) as they enter viewport. No looping motion here —
this section is a calm, confident pause after the hero's energy.

## What to avoid
No stock "our mission" icon grid (target/eye/handshake icons), no repeating the
brochure's literal paragraph verbatim, no second hero-style big image.
