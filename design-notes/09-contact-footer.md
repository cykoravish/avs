# Section skill: Contact + Footer

## Job
Convert. Everything before this was persuasion; this is the single action the
whole page has been building toward. Keep friction low — short form, real
contact details from the brochure, no scroll needed to see the whole thing on
desktop.

## Structure — `--ink` closing band
- Left (45%): big display line "Let's talk high street." + direct contact block
  (phone, email placeholder, site address) in mono, plus office hours line. This
  is the "you don't even need the form" path for a serious buyer.
- Right (55%): compact enquiry form on a `--paper` card floating over the ink
  band (name, phone, email, interest — select: Retail/Hospitality/Office —,
  message), brass submit button. Front-end only for now — on submit, shows an
  inline confirmation state (no backend wired yet, flagged clearly to the dev).

## Footer (separate, below, `--ink`, darker hairline top)
Slim: wordmark, quick nav links (same 6), social placeholders, address +
disclaimer line adapted from brochure ("Images used are for artistic impression..."),
copyright. Single row on desktop, stacked on mobile. No new visual language —
this just closes the page quietly.

## Motion
Form card: fade/rise on scroll-in. Submit button: brief inline success state
morph (icon + "Thanks, we'll call you back" text swap), no page navigation.

## What to avoid
No multi-step form, no map embed repeated here (already shown in Location).
